The U.S. Energy Information Administration is the statistical agency of the US Department of Energy, and the single largest source of data here. It publishes the same numbers through two independent channels — bulk spreadsheet downloads and a keyed JSON API — which is what makes it possible to check a publisher against itself. Most of the problems this project has caught were found that way.

Lower 48 gas storage, and the five regions behind it

EIA's Weekly Natural Gas Storage Report is the number this market watches: how much gas is in underground storage across the Lower 48 — the contiguous states, which behave as one connected pipeline system. Each value is for the week ending Friday and is published the following Thursday at 10:30 ET.

The total is also published as five regions. They are not merely decoration: the South Central region holds the salt caverns, which can be filled and emptied far faster than depleted gas fields, so it moves most in a cold snap, while the East and Midwest are where the winter heating demand actually is.
